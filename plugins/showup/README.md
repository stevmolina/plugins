# Show Up

Turns real work into personal social content in Steven's voice. Needs [`steven-core`](../steven-core) for the voice and approval rules.

- `showup-capture` saves evidence and story material to a Notion Content Inbox.
- `showup-draft` turns grounded material into X and LinkedIn drafts.
- `showup-publish` moves approved copy through Typefully.
- `showup-review` reviews results and improves the content system.

The skills expect a Notion workspace with a Content Inbox and a connected Typefully account, and they look both up live. Install it from the [marketplace](../../README.md).
